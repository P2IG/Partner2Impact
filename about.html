Common questions

Before you reach out

Is using donor data for predictive targeting ethically sound and compliant with privacy laws?
Data ethics and security are central to this framework. We work exclusively with aggregated, anonymized, and privacy-compliant datasets, adhering strictly to GDPR, CCPA, and donor privacy standards. Furthermore, when we upload prospect cohorts to media platforms like Meta or LinkedIn, the data is heavily encrypted and hashed. We never sell donor information.
Do we have to change our CRM?
No. The tool layers on top of the system you already use — Raiser’s Edge, Salesforce, Bloomerang, DonorPerfect and others. There is no migration project.
Our database is a mess. Is it too early for this?
That is the normal starting point, and cleaning it is step one of the work. The data health check up will tell you honestly whether the records are good enough to model yet, and what it would take to get there.
How does an engagement start?
With a free 15-minute data health check up. You see a live demonstration and get an honest read on whether we are the right fit.
How long does setup take, and will it disrupt our daily workflow?
Zero downtime for your team. Getting started takes less than 15 minutes. There is no software to install or agonizing database overhaul required — simply export your existing donor data or connect your CRM, and our system generates your customized blueprint.
Is our donor data secure and compliant?
Yes, security is our top priority. We use bank-level encryption (256-bit SSL) to protect your records both in transit and at rest. We never sell, share, or monetize your donor data, and all analysis complies strictly with global privacy standards.
What CRMs or database platforms do you support?
We work with virtually any system. Whether you use Salesforce NPSP, Raiser’s Edge, Bloomerang, Neon One, DonorPerfect, or even organized Excel spreadsheets, our analysis engine seamlessly ingests your data.
We are a small team with limited bandwidth. Will this give us more work?
Quite the opposite — it saves time. Instead of giving you overwhelming dashboards, we deliver direct, prioritized action lists. You’ll know precisely which 10 donors to call today rather than spending hours trying to figure out where to focus.
How quickly will we see results?
Most organizations identify high-value opportunities within 30 days of receiving their initial blueprint. Many clients cover their annual investment with the very first hidden major gift unlocked.
